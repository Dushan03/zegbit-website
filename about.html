Engineers who build it and break it.

Zegbit is a Colombo-based team of software, security and network engineers. We build systems and we attack them, so the ones we hand over are ready for the real world.

Zeg + bit.

Zeg comes from zetta, the largest scale of data. Bit is the smallest unit. From a single bit to zetta scale, we look after all of it.

  • Outer layer. The perimeter. Your first line of defence.
  • Middle layer. The network. Controlled, reliable connections.
  • Lime core. What matters. Your data and your business.
  • The Z cut. One line through every layer. We work at every level.
