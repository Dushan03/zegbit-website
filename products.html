ZegBit PDPA Ready

Get ready for Sri Lanka’s data protection law.

The Personal Data Protection Act No. 9 of 2022 asks every business that holds personal information to know what it holds, why, and how it keeps it safe. Answer simple questions and PDPA Ready produces your documents and an action plan.

  • Free readiness check
  • Guided data inventory: what you hold, why, where and who can see it
  • Document pack: privacy notice, data protection policy, retention schedule, breach response plan, supplier agreement template
  • Readiness score out of 100 with a prioritised fix list
  • Staff awareness lessons in three languages, plus a breach log
A readiness tool, not legal adviceTemplates reviewed by a qualified Sri Lankan lawyer
